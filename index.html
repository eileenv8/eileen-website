About

A generalist who's good with groups.

I studied economics at Reed College, with a focus on sociology and anthropology. Since then I've facilitated work parties, built community programs, written strategy and research, and worked with AI since 2019, starting at Credit Suisse Labs.

Most recently I was Membership Manager at The Fold, a community workspace in San Francisco's Mission District. Before that, I lived and worked at Elkenmist, a regenerative farm in Washington.

I run Jubilee Studio with my husband, Calvin.
